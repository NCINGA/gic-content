## Page 1

Registration of Sri Lankan Citizenship for Children Born 
Outside Sri Lanka 
Overview 
A child born outside Sri Lanka may be registered as a Sri Lankan citizen when at least one 
parent is a Sri Lankan citizen. Parents are advised to complete the registration within one year 
from the child’s date of birth. 
The Citizenship Division of the Department of Immigration and Emigration processes these 
applications and issues the relevant citizenship registration certificate. 
Who Can Apply? 
The child’s father or mother may submit the application, provided that at least one parent held 
Sri Lankan citizenship at the time of the child’s birth. 
When the application is submitted after one year from the date of birth, an additional fee will be 
charged for each year of delay. 
Required Documents 
The following documents should be submitted with the completed application: 
1.​ The child’s original birth certificate issued by the country of birth. 
2.​ An English translation if the original birth certificate is not issued in English. 
3.​ The Consular Birth Certificate issued by the Registrar General’s Department of Sri 
Lanka. 
4.​ The original birth certificates of both parents. 
5.​ The relevant citizenship certificate if the Sri Lankan parent obtained citizenship by 
registration. 
6.​ The original marriage certificate of the parents. 
7.​ The parents’ travel documents and visas used at the time of the child’s birth. 
8.​ Dual citizenship certificates, where either parent holds dual citizenship. 
How to Apply 
A duly completed application may be submitted through one of the following methods: 
●​ Directly to the Citizenship Division at the Head Office of the Department of Immigration 
and Emigration, “Suhurupaya”, Battaramulla. 
●​ Through the Sri Lankan Embassy or Diplomatic Mission in the country where the child 
was born.

Applications submitted through a Sri Lankan Diplomatic Mission must include the required 
documents certified by an authorized officer of the mission. 
Applicable Fees 
●​ Application submitted within one year of birth: LKR 5,750 
●​ Additional fee for a delayed application: LKR 500 for each year of delay 
●​ Duplicate citizenship registration certificate: LKR 2,000 
The applicable fee must be paid when submitting the application. Applicants should retain the 
payment receipt for future reference. 
Receiving the Certificate 
Once the application has been processed, the citizenship registration certificate may be: 
●​ Collected through the relevant Sri Lankan Embassy or Diplomatic Mission; 
●​ Sent by registered post to the applicant’s address in Sri Lanka; or 
●​ Collected by one of the child’s parents from the Citizenship Division.
