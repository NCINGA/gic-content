# School Examinations in Sri Lanka

## **Overview**

School examinations are an important part of the education system in Sri Lanka. They are conducted to assess students’ knowledge, skills and academic performance at different stages of their school education. National-level examinations are conducted according to procedures and regulations established by the relevant education authorities.

The main national examinations conducted for school students include the Grade 5 Scholarship Examination, the General Certificate of Education (Ordinary Level) Examination and the General Certificate of Education (Advanced Level) Examination.

## **Main School Examinations**

### Grade 5 Scholarship Examination
- Grade: Grade 5
- Purpose: To assess students’ academic performance and provide opportunities for further educational development.

### G.C.E. (Ordinary Level) Examination
- Grade: Grade 11
- Purpose: To assess students at the secondary education level and determine eligibility for advanced-level studies.

### G.C.E. (Advanced Level) Examination
- Grade: Grade 13
- Purpose: To assess students at the senior secondary education level and support entry to higher education and other opportunities.

## **Examination Application Process**

Students who are eligible to sit for a national examination should complete the relevant application process within the period specified by the examination authorities.

The general process includes:

Check the examination eligibility requirements.

Obtain or access the examination application through the relevant official channel.

Enter the required personal and educational information accurately.

Select the relevant examination subjects, where applicable.

Submit the application within the specified period.

Keep a record of the submitted application for future reference.

Students should ensure that the information provided in the application is accurate. Incorrect or incomplete information may affect the examination application.

## **Before the Examination**

Students should make the necessary preparations before appearing for the examination.

Check the examination timetable and examination dates.

Ensure that the admission card is available.

Check the examination centre mentioned in the admission card.

Prepare the required stationery and permitted materials.

Arrive at the examination centre before the specified reporting time.

Follow all instructions issued by the examination authorities.

## **During the Examination**

Students are required to comply with the examination rules and instructions throughout the examination.

Enter the examination hall according to the instructions of the examination staff.

Keep the admission card and required identification documents available.

Follow the instructions given by the examination supervisor.

Use only permitted materials during the examination.

Do not communicate with other candidates during the examination.

Do not engage in any activity that violates examination rules.

Hand over the answer scripts according to the instructions before leaving the examination hall.

## **Examination Results**

Examination results are released through official channels after the completion of the relevant examination and the processing of examination results. Students can obtain their results using the methods announced by the relevant authorities.

Students should use official sources when checking examination results and avoid relying on unofficial information.

## **Important Information for Students and Parents**

Students and parents are advised to:

Follow announcements issued by the relevant education authorities.

Check examination dates and deadlines carefully.

Keep admission cards and other examination-related documents safely.

Ensure that applications are submitted within the specified period.

Follow examination rules and regulations.

Refer to official government sources for the latest information.

Examination dates, application procedures, fees, eligibility requirements and other arrangements may change from time to time. Students and parents should refer to the latest announcements issued by the relevant authorities.
