Department of Immigration and Emigration 
1. About the Department 
The Department of Immigration and Emigration is the government department responsible for 
regulating the entry of persons into Sri Lanka and the departure of persons from Sri Lanka. It 
also provides passport services to Sri Lankan citizens, manages citizenship-related services, 
and provides immigration and emigration services to foreign nationals. 
 
2. Main Services 
The Department of Immigration and Emigration provides the following major services: 
●​ Passport services 
●​ Visa services 
●​ Visa extensions 
●​ Citizenship-related services 
●​ Dual citizenship 
●​ Renunciation of Sri Lankan citizenship 
●​ Registration of children born overseas 
●​ Border management services 
●​ Immigration and emigration inquiries 
●​ Travel document-related services 
 
3. Passport Services 
The Department provides various passport-related services for Sri Lankan citizens. 
These include: 
●​ Applying for a new passport 
●​ Renewing a passport 
●​ Handling lost passports 
●​ Handling stolen passports 
●​ Amending passport information 
●​ Obtaining separate passports for children

●​ Official passports 
●​ Diplomatic passports 
●​ Passport services for Sri Lankan citizens overseas 
 
4. Applying for a New Passport 
Applicants must complete the relevant application form and submit it together with the required 
supporting documents. 
Form K 35 A is used for passport applications. 
The documents required may vary depending on the applicant's circumstances. 
 
5. Documents Required for a Passport 
Depending on the circumstances, the following documents may be required: 
●​ Original birth certificate 
●​ National Identity Card 
●​ Current passport 
●​ Photo studio acknowledgement 
●​ Marriage certificate 
●​ Documents confirming a change of name or profession 
●​ Other relevant supporting documents 
Applicants should check the latest official requirements before applying because the required 
documents may vary according to the applicant's circumstances. 
 
6. Passport Photographs 
Applicants must provide a digital photograph through an authorized photo studio when applying 
for a passport. 
Applicants between 16 and 60 years of age are required to provide fingerprints at the relevant 
offices.

7. Lost or Stolen Passport 
If a passport is lost or stolen, the incident should be reported to the relevant authorities and the 
applicant should follow the instructions provided by the Department of Immigration and 
Emigration. 
Additional documents may be required when applying for a replacement passport. 
 
8. Changing Passport Information 
If an applicant needs to change the name, profession, or other information in a passport, the 
applicant should submit the relevant application together with documents supporting the 
requested change. 
For a name change following marriage, the relevant marriage certificate may be required. 
 
9. Passports for Children 
The Department provides passport-related services for children. 
When applying for a passport for a child, documents such as the child's birth certificate and 
relevant documents of the parents may be required. 
The required documents may vary depending on the child's circumstances. 
 
10. Visa Services 
The Department of Immigration and Emigration provides visa-related services for foreign 
nationals entering and staying in Sri Lanka. 
Major visa services include: 
●​ Tourist visas 
●​ Business visas 
●​ Transit visas 
●​ Visa extensions 
●​ Visa-related inquiries

11. Visa Extension 
A foreign national who needs to extend their period of stay in Sri Lanka must follow the official 
procedure of the Department of Immigration and Emigration applicable to their visa category. 
Eligibility requirements, required documents, and fees may vary depending on the type of visa. 
Applicants should refer to the latest official information before applying. 
 
12. Citizenship Services 
The Citizenship Division of the Department provides various citizenship-related services. 
These include: 
●​ Registration of children born overseas 
●​ Dual citizenship 
●​ Renunciation of Sri Lankan citizenship 
●​ Citizenship-related certificates and other services 
 
13. Dual Citizenship 
The Citizenship Division provides dual citizenship services for eligible persons who have 
obtained or intend to obtain citizenship of another country while retaining or resuming Sri 
Lankan citizenship. 
Applicants must submit the relevant application and required supporting documents. 
The required documents and eligibility criteria may vary depending on the applicant's category 
and circumstances. 
 
14. Registration of Children Born Overseas 
The Citizenship Division provides services related to the registration of children born overseas 
to Sri Lankan parents.

Applicants must follow the relevant procedure and submit the required application and 
supporting documents. 
The required documents may vary depending on the circumstances of the child and parents. 
 
15. Renunciation of Sri Lankan Citizenship 
Persons who wish to renounce Sri Lankan citizenship must follow the relevant procedure 
through the Citizenship Division of the Department of Immigration and Emigration. 
The applicant must submit the relevant application and required supporting documents. 
 
16. Border Management 
The Department manages immigration and emigration activities involving Sri Lankan citizens 
and foreign nationals entering and leaving Sri Lanka. 
Border management activities are carried out through approved ports of entry and exit, including 
relevant airports and other authorized locations.
