Divisional Secretariat – Common Manual 
1. Introduction 
A Divisional Secretariat is a government administrative institution that provides and 
coordinates public services at the divisional level. 
Divisional Secretariats support the implementation of government policies, administrative 
activities, social welfare programmes, development programmes, land-related activities, and 
other public services within their respective administrative divisions. 
Citizens can contact the relevant Divisional Secretariat to obtain information about government 
services available in their area. 
 
2. Main Services 
The services provided or coordinated through Divisional Secretariats may include: 
●​ Administrative services 
●​ Land-related services 
●​ Government land services 
●​ Social welfare services 
●​ Government assistance programmes 
●​ Disaster relief services 
●​ Grama Niladhari-related services 
●​ Certificates and recommendations 
●​ Livelihood development programmes 
●​ Senior citizen services 
●​ Services for persons with disabilities 
●​ Child protection services 
●​ Women's development programmes 
●​ Public complaints 
●​ Community development activities 
●​ Local development programmes 
The availability of a particular service may depend on the relevant Divisional Secretariat and 
applicable government procedures.

3. Grama Niladhari Services 
A Grama Niladhari (GN) is a government officer who provides administrative services to the 
public at the local level. 
Citizens may need to contact the relevant GN Officer for services such as: 
●​ Confirmation of residence 
●​ Certificates 
●​ Recommendations 
●​ Local administrative matters 
●​ Government welfare-related activities 
●​ Other services assigned to the GN Officer 
The required documents and procedures depend on the service requested. 
 
4. Finding the Relevant Grama Niladhari 
The relevant Grama Niladhari depends on the citizen's residential address and GN Division. 
Citizens should provide their correct residential area when requesting information about their 
GN Officer. 
If the citizen is unsure which GN Division they belong to, they may contact the relevant 
Divisional Secretariat for assistance. 
 
5. Residence Confirmation 
A citizen may require confirmation of residence for various government or administrative 
purposes. 
Depending on the requirement, the citizen may need to obtain a certificate or recommendation 
from the relevant Grama Niladhari. 
The documents required may include: 
●​ National Identity Card 
●​ Proof of address 
●​ Relevant certificates 
●​ Other supporting documents

The exact requirements depend on the purpose of the residence confirmation. 
 
6. Certificates and Recommendations 
Citizens may require certificates or recommendations for government, educational, employment, 
legal, financial, or other administrative purposes. 
The responsible authority depends on the type of certificate or recommendation. 
Citizens should confirm: 
●​ The responsible authority 
●​ Required documents 
●​ Application procedure 
●​ Applicable fees 
●​ Processing requirements 
before submitting an application. 
 
7. Birth-Related Services 
Birth registration and birth certificate services are handled through the relevant registration 
authorities. 
A citizen who requires a birth certificate or a copy of a birth certificate should provide the 
relevant birth registration information. 
The procedure may depend on: 
●​ Place of birth 
●​ Registration details 
●​ Date of registration 
●​ Availability of records 
Citizens should contact the relevant registration authority for the current procedure.

26. Before Visiting a Divisional Secretariat 
Citizens should: 
1.​ Identify the required service. 
2.​ Identify the relevant Divisional Secretariat. 
3.​ Confirm the required documents. 
4.​ Obtain the relevant application form if applicable. 
5.​ Confirm office hours. 
6.​ Confirm whether an appointment is required. 
7.​ Confirm whether a government fee applies. 
8.​ Carry original documents when verification is required. 
9.​ Carry copies of important documents when necessary.
