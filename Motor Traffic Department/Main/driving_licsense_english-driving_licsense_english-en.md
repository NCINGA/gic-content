# DRIVING Licsense-English

## Q1: What is the minimum age to apply for a driving licence in Sri Lanka?

You generally need to be 18 years old to obtain a full driving licence.

## Q2: Q: What do I need to get a learner’s permit?


A: You typically need a valid National Identity Card or passport, pass the required medical examination, and complete the necessary application process.

## Q3: Q: Do I need to pass a written test?


A: Yes. Applicants generally need to pass a written/theory test covering road rules, traffic signs, and safe driving practices before proceeding to the practical test.

## Q4: Q: What is required for the practical driving test?


A: You must demonstrate that you can safely control the vehicle and follow traffic rules, including proper starting, stopping, turning, reversing, and other required driving skills.

## Q5: Q: Can I drive alone with a learner’s permit?


A: No. A learner should follow the conditions of the learner’s permit and be accompanied/supervised as required by Sri Lankan regulations. A learner’s permit is not the same as a full driving licence.
