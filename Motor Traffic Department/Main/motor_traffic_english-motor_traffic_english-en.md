1. Department Overview 
The Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) is the government department responsible for the 
administration and regulation of motor vehicles and driving licences in Sri Lanka. Its services 
include vehicle registration, driving licences, ownership transfers, vehicle information, revenue 
licence-related services and other motor-traffic services. 
The department provides services through its Head Office, Werahera Office and district-level 
offices.

6. Light Vehicle Driving Licence 
For a new light-vehicle driving licence: 
●​ The applicant can register and sit for the written examination after completing 17 years 
of age. 
●​ After passing the written examination, a learner's permit can be issued for a maximum 
period of 18 months. 
●​ The applicant must complete 18 years of age to face the practical examination.

## Page 4

●​ At least three months should have passed after obtaining the learner's permit before 
facing the practical examination. 
●​ The applicant must pass the practical examination. 
●​ An Aptitude Medical Certificate from the National Transport Medical Institution, obtained 
within six months, is required.

For first registration of a motor vehicle in Sri Lanka: 
●​ The vehicle should not have been previously registered in Sri Lanka. 
●​ Left-hand-drive vehicles are not registered in Sri Lanka. 
●​ The MTA-2 application should be completed in English. 
●​ Relevant documents can be submitted to the relevant registration branch at the DMT 
Head Office in Narahenpita on working days from 9.00 a.m. to 1.30 p.m. 
●​ Only motor bicycles are registered at DMT branches of District Secretariats. 
●​ Application forms are issued free of charge.
