5.0 Challenges Encountered 
During this month, I encountered several technical, process-related, and communication 
challenges while working on the ICTA GIC CMS Portal and Citizen Portal. These challenges helped 
me gain practical experience in software quality assurance and improved my problem-solving, 
analytical thinking, and collaboration skills. 
• Technical Challenges 
One of the main technical challenges was testing applications in an environment where frequent 
deployments and UI changes were taking place. As new fixes and enhancements were deployed 
regularly, I had to repeatedly perform regression testing to ensure that existing functionalities 
continued to work correctly without introducing new defects. 
Another challenge was verifying defects when some functionalities depended on backend 
services or unresolved issues. In such situations, certain bugs could not be verified immediately 
and required multiple rounds of retesting after the dependent issues were resolved. 
Additionally, I began preparing for the CEFT project by attending Knowledge Transfer (KT) 
sessions and reviewing project documentation. Since the banking and reconciliation domain was 
new to me, understanding the business processes and system workflows required additional 
learning and clarification. 
• Process-Related Challenges 
Understanding new requirements and converting them into comprehensive test scenarios 
required careful analysis and attention to detail. As the applications evolved through continuous 
development, I needed to update my testing approach to ensure all functional and UI changes 
were properly validated. 
Managing multiple responsibilities, including functional testing, UI testing, regression testing, bug 
reporting, bug verification, release validation, and CEFT learning activities, also required effective 
time management and prioritization. Proper planning helped me complete assigned tasks while 
maintaining the quality of testing.

I also participated in CEFT Knowledge Transfer (KT) sessions and project discussions, which

6.0 Root Cause Analysis of Challenges 
 
The challenges encountered during this month were analyzed to identify their underlying causes 
and to better understand how similar situations can be managed more effectively in future 
projects. 
• Frequent Changes in the Testing Environment 
One of the primary causes of repeated testing activities was the continuous deployment of new 
features, UI improvements, and bug fixes to the QA environment. As the ​application was under 
active development, frequent updates required repeated regression testing and bug verification 
to ensure that newly introduced changes did not ​ affect existing functionalities. 
• Dependencies Between System Components 
Several testing activities depended on backend services, APIs, or other unresolved defects. As a 
result, some issues could not be verified immediately and required waiting for dependent fixes to 
be completed before retesting. These dependencies occasionally delayed the testing process.
